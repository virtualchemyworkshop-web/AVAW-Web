01 — WHAT WE DO

A preproduction workshop.

We work the stage before the camera rolls: storyboards that plan a shoot shot by shot, previs environments built in Unreal Engine, and animatics or techvis as the final deliverable. We're glad to stay involved as coordinators through the later stages of a production, but the content we build stays in preproduction — we don't produce on-set or post work ourselves.

02 — SERVICES

Three disciplines, one preproduction.

SEQUENCE · STORYBOARD

Storyboard

Sequential visual storytelling for film, games and previs. Shot composition, continuity and camera language, carried from rough thumbnail through to animatic-ready boards.

UE · PREVIS & LEVEL DESIGN

Previs Level Design — Unreal Engine

Blockout and previs environments built in Unreal Engine: layout, blocking, lighting studies and camera blocking, taken through to animatics or techvis-ready scenes.

ICVFX · STAGECRAFT DESIGN

ICVFX Stagecraft Design Assistance

Support through the creation and design process of a Stagecraft setup: layout planning, camera and content placement, and recommendations on LED panel technology — brand and type — suited to the project.
